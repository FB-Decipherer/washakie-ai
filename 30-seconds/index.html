Project Details

What the 30 seconds left out. Figures are from washakie-ai.online and the format’s own reference.

Better than a Facebook page

  • Anyone can read it. No account, no sign-in wall, no “see more” prompts.
  • Superior branding: no ads or rival businesses placed beside it.
  • Found by search engines as a real website, at the owner’s own web address.
  • Laid out for answers: hours, directions and prices stay in place, not buried under an ever-expanding article feed.
  • Can’t be switched off by a platform’s rule change or an account suspension.
  • Downloads as a PDF for printing or handing out.

What it costs

  • Hosting: $0. Plain files on GitHub Pages, with the certificate issued automatically.
  • The name: about $11–$28 a year under the owner’s custom URL, or free under a shared name such as gyro-sites.online or a farm cooperative’s.
  • The AI: one Claude plan for the Site Builder, $17 a month billed yearly. The owner pays nothing for it.
  • For comparison: the AI website builders charge about $17–$49 a month, and the site disappears when the payments stop.

Security: nothing to attack

  • No database, so no records to leak.
  • No logins, so no passwords to steal.
  • No forms and no server code, so nothing to break into.
  • No plugins, so nothing to fall out of date.
  • No cookies, analytics or tracking, so no visitor data held.
  • What’s left to guard: the publishing account and the domain account, both protected with two-factor sign-in.

Ownership

  • The site is a few plain files. Copy the folder to any other host, point the name at it, and the site is identical.
  • No proprietary format, no theme to rebuild, nothing to export.
  • Every site is saved to the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine.

The pilot

  • 12 months, about $10,000.
  • 12 working sites and 4 public workshops in Washakie County.
  • High school ambassadors demonstrate. An adult (18+) Site Builder orchestrates provisioning the website, as Claude accounts require.
  • The technical scheme is freely published.

Already working: nine sites

  • worland-dog-law.online: Worland’s dog ordinances in plain language, every claim linked to the code.
  • washakie-ai.online: the proposal, itself built in the format.
  • gyro-sites.online: the format itself.
  • baconian-ciphers.online: a dated record of University-level research.
  • Five samples: East Bench Pivot Farm, Cottonwood Draw Farms (also in Spanish), Ruby Calder, Bighorn Tree Service, and an Ambassador’s own page.
